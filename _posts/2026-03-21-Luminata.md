## Luminata

Fall means the darkening of days and the coming of rain. For Art Haus and
the [Fremont Arts Council](https://fremontartscouncil.org/luminata) it means
celebrating the autumnal equinox with lanterns and the lighting of Greenlake!

This is our fourth year making lanterns and bringing art to this celebration.
We made a few lanterns while helping out at Powerhouse workshops, and choreographed a fire performance for the end of the lantern procession.
